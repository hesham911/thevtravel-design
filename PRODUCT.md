# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Travelers planning a private journey or transfer in Egypt who want clear, human help before committing to a booking.

## Product Purpose

TheVTravel helps travelers discover private Egypt journeys and transfers, understand how booking works, and send a request for human confirmation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Journeys and private transfers are requested online and confirmed by a person.
- The FAQ is local frontend content; no backend FAQ service is required.
- No payment or deposit is taken online.
- Contact details must not be invented when no verified contact is configured.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: TheVTravel.
- Existing logo assets and shared site components are authoritative.
- The approved FAQ screenshot at `/Users/hanafawy/Downloads/thevtraveldesign /FAQ.png` is the visual source of truth for `/faq`.
- Product language is calm, clear, personal, and locally informed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing Home and About implementations establish the shared frontend conventions.
- Existing Egypt travel photography and brand assets are stored in `public/assets`.
- No verified WhatsApp contact URL is currently present in the project.

## Product Principles

- Keep booking guidance transparent and easy to scan.
- Preserve human confirmation as a core part of the experience.
- Reuse shared site foundations without changing unrelated pages.
- Never fabricate contact information or commercial claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive controls use semantic elements, visible focus states, and explicit expanded/current states.
